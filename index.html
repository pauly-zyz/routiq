FAQ

Common questions.

What is ROUTIQ?

A routing layer for Robinhood Chain. It compares prices across the chain's trading venues and splits your order to get the best overall execution in a single transaction.

Which network does it run on?

Robinhood Chain mainnet — an Ethereum Layer 2 built on Arbitrum Orbit. Chain ID 4663, gas is paid in ETH, and you can track transactions on the Blockscout explorer.

Which wallets are supported?

Any browser wallet that supports the standard Ethereum provider, such as MetaMask, Rabby or Robinhood Wallet. ROUTIQ adds Robinhood Chain to your wallet when you connect.

Can everyone trade stock tokens?

Stock token eligibility is set by the issuer and depends on where you live. ROUTIQ only routes assets your wallet is allowed to hold and does not bypass those rules.

Is there a ROUTIQ token?

The official contract address is only published on this site and our official social accounts. Never trust addresses sent in DMs.

Run a pool on Robinhood Chain?

Plug your AMM, order book or vault into ROUTIQ and every trade routed through it can reach your liquidity.
